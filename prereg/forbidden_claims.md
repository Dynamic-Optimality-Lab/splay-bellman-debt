# Forbidden claims (from spec sections 25-26, condensed)
- V_b is the universal closed-form Splay potential.
- A correlating atom is therefore the universal potential.
- Kernel insufficiency proves no structural potential of that general kind can exist (unless class exhausted).
- Same-(A,B) recency spread in V proves history is necessary (false; V is provably recency-blind).
- Holdout survival means the conjecture is probably solved.
- Candidate failure means dynamic optimality is false.
- Fixed-b positive-regret cycle disproves dynamic optimality.
- Finite Bellman tables, feature importance, holdout survival, or numerical patterns are universal claims.
